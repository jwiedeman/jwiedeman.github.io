# Message-Market Match

Message match means the landing page repeats the headline, offer, and look of the ad, email, or link that sent the visitor. If the ad says "50% off running shoes," the landing page should say "50% off running shoes" right away. A generic homepage makes visitors think they are in the wrong place.

## How it works

Every click carries an expectation set by the ad. When the landing page shows a different headline, offer, or style, visitors feel lost and leave. When the page matches, each step confirms they are in the right place, and they keep going.

## How to do it

1. List every active traffic source: ads, emails, social posts, partner links.
2. For each one, record the headline, offer, and visual style in a spreadsheet next to its landing page.
3. Give each source a landing page or page variant whose headline repeats the ad copy.
4. Match the hero image to the ad's look and restate the offer in the CTA.
5. Use dynamic text replacement (Unbounce, Instapage, or a small script) to swap the headline based on UTM parameters.
6. Match tone as well as words. A casual ad should lead to a casual page.
7. A/B test the matched page against a generic page. Compare bounce rate and conversion rate.

## What to measure

- **Bounce rate by source**: Bounce rate for each ad or email. A high rate on one source points to a mismatch.
- **Quality Score / relevance**: Google Ads Quality Score and similar platform ratings. Better landing page relevance can lower cost per click.
- **Conversion rate by ad-page pair**: Conversion rate for each ad and landing page combination.

## Best practices

- Reuse the ad's exact wording in the headline. Do not paraphrase.
- Match the emotion. If the ad mentions a deadline, the page should show the same deadline.
- Carry the promise through the CTA, checkout, and confirmation email.

## Common pitfalls

- Sending all traffic to the homepage because building pages takes time. This wastes the ad spend.
- Matching the headline but not the body. If the headline says "Free trial" and the body opens with pricing, visitors still get confused.
- Not updating landing pages when ads change. Old offers on the page create a mismatch.
