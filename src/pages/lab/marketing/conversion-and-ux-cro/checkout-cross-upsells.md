# Checkout Cross-Sells & Upsells

Checkout cross-sells and upsells show extra offers after a customer decides to buy but before they pay. A cross-sell adds a related item ("Add a case for your phone?"). An upsell swaps in a better version ("Get the Pro model for $20 more").

## How it works

At checkout the customer has already decided to buy. Adding one more relevant item takes little extra effort. The risk is distraction: every offer competes with finishing the purchase. Offers must be relevant, few, and easy to accept or skip.

## How to do it

1. Pull order history and list which products are most often bought together.
2. List which products customers buy next in later orders.
3. Show two or three related items on the cart page with one-click "Add" buttons that keep the customer in checkout.
4. Present upsells as a price difference, not a new total. "$20 more for double the storage" is clearer than "Upgrade to the $299 model."
5. Offer one small add-on at the final step, such as gift wrap, a warranty, or faster shipping. Use a checkbox.
6. Start with one upsell and one cross-sell. Add more only if checkout completion holds steady.

## What to measure

- **AOV lift**: Average order value for orders with an accepted offer versus orders without, or a test group versus a control group.
- **Offer acceptance rate**: Share of checkouts where the customer adds at least one suggested item.
- **Checkout completion rate**: Compare with and without offers. A drop means the offers add friction.

## Best practices

- Recommend items that complete the main purchase: a case with a phone, batteries with a toy.
- Keep offers in one contained section that does not push the pay button down.
- Use real data for social proof on offers, such as "Often bought together."

## Common pitfalls

- Filling checkout with banners and carousels. Checkout exists to finish the sale.
- Suggesting items that cost more than what is in the cart. Keep add-ons well below the main item's price.
- Showing the same "bestsellers" to everyone instead of items related to the cart.
