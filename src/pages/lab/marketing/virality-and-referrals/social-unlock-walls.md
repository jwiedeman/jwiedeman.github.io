# Social Unlock Walls

A social unlock wall (also called "share to unlock" or "pay with a tweet") gives access to content, a discount, a download, or a feature after the user shares a post on social media. Each unlock places a post about you in the user's feed. Check each platform's rules first: some prohibit rewarding people for sharing, and most no longer let third parties confirm that a share happened.

## How it works

The user pays with reach instead of money. A post from someone you know is more trusted than an ad. The exchange only works if the user thinks the content is worth the share.

## How to do it

1. Create an asset people actually want: a template, a detailed guide, a discount code, early access, or a tool result.
2. Keep the flow short: show a preview of what is locked, offer a "Share to unlock" button for 2 or 3 platforms, prefill the post text and image, and deliver the content right after.
3. Review the current terms of each platform you use. Where rewarding shares is not allowed, or a share cannot be confirmed, use an email signup as the unlock instead.
4. Test different assets and offers. A specific deliverable ("Download the 50-page salary negotiation playbook") usually beats a vague one ("Access exclusive content").
5. Add UTM parameters to shared links and track the traffic and signups they bring.

## What to measure

- **Unlock completion rate** — users who complete the share divided by users who saw the wall.
- **Cost per reach** — cost of creating the asset divided by impressions from shared posts, compared with your paid social CPM.
- **Referral traffic from shares** — visits and signups from the tagged links in shared posts.

## Best practices

- Always offer another way in: email signup, purchase, or waiting. Some people cannot or will not post.
- Write share text that sounds like a person, for example "Found a useful salary negotiation template," not "Check out [Brand]'s amazing new resource!"
- Show the wall after a free preview or other sign of interest, not on first contact.

## Common pitfalls

- Gating weak content. If the unlocked content disappoints, users regret the share and think less of you.
- Using walls everywhere. People learn to avoid your content.
- Spammy prefilled text. Users delete it or skip sharing, and the posts that do go out get little engagement.
