# Product Hunt Launch Orchestration

Product Hunt is a site where early adopters discover and vote on new products. It suits SaaS, developer tools, and other digital products. A planned launch can bring signups, backlinks, and social proof in one day. Products near the top of the daily list get most of the attention, so preparation matters more than the submission itself.

## How it works

Product Hunt runs a daily leaderboard. Products launched that day compete for upvotes and comments, and the top products get the best homepage placement and appear in the newsletter. Product Hunt does not publish its full ranking method and filters out votes it sees as coordinated. A good launch combines a clear listing, an audience told in advance, and a maker team that answers comments all day.

## How to do it

1. Prepare the listing a few weeks ahead: a short tagline, a plain description, gallery images or GIFs of the product in use, an optional short demo video, and a first comment from the maker.
2. Decide who submits. You can launch it yourself or ask an active community member to hunt it.
3. Tell your email list, network, and social followers the launch date. Ask them to look and leave honest feedback. Do not ask for upvotes.
4. Launch at 12:01 AM Pacific, when the Product Hunt day starts.
5. Staff the listing for the full day and reply to every comment.
6. Offer a launch-only deal, such as an extended trial, and track signups with a UTM-tagged link.

## What to measure

- **Daily rank**: your final position on the leaderboard for launch day.
- **Upvotes and comments**: totals on the listing at the end of the day.
- **Signups from launch**: signups attributed to the Product Hunt UTM link during the launch window and the following week.

## Best practices

- Pick a day that avoids major competing launches. Weekday traffic is usually higher, and so is the competition.
- Write a personal maker comment: why you built it, what problem it solves, and what feedback you want.
- Reply to each comment specifically rather than with generic thanks.
- Stay active on Product Hunt after the launch.

## Common pitfalls

- Launching with no audience told in advance, so the listing gets no early attention.
- Asking people to create accounts and upvote together, which can get votes removed.
- Treating launch day as the end. Follow up with the people who signed up and commented.
