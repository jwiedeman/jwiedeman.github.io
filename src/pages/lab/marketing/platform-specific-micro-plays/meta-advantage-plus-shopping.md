# Meta Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns

Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (ASC) were Meta's automated campaign type for e-commerce. In 2025 Meta folded them into Advantage+ sales campaigns, which follow the same idea. Meta handles audience, placement, and creative combinations in one campaign across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and Audience Network.

## How it works

Instead of building audiences by hand, you give Meta a broad market and let its system decide who sees each ad, where, and when. It uses your catalog, your Pixel and Conversions API data, and its own models to find likely buyers. Your main job becomes supplying creative: many different ads so the system has options to match to different people.

## How to do it

1. Set up your product catalog in Commerce Manager with accurate titles, descriptions, prices, images, and categories.
2. In Ads Manager, create a campaign with the Sales objective and use the Advantage+ setup. Optimize for Purchase.
3. Upload a wide range of creative: static images, carousels, video, and UGC, with different hooks and angles.
4. Upload customer lists so Meta can tell new customers from existing ones, then check the current audience settings for controlling spend on existing customers.
5. Let the campaign run for one to two weeks before judging it. Early results are unstable during the learning phase.

## What to measure

- **Cost per purchase (CPA)**: spend divided by purchases, compared with your manually targeted campaigns.
- **ROAS**: purchase value divided by spend, at campaign and ad level.
- **New customer share**: purchases from new customers divided by all purchases, from the new vs existing customer breakdown.

## Best practices

- Supply many creative variations across formats, hooks, and messages. A handful of ads is not enough.
- Watch the new vs existing customer split and adjust settings if spend drifts toward people who already buy.
- Keep one or two manual campaigns for promotions or audiences that need precise control.

## Common pitfalls

- Launching with too few creative assets, which gives results no better than a manual campaign.
- Ignoring the existing customer share, which inflates ROAS with sales that would have happened anyway.
- Killing the campaign during the learning phase before results settle.
