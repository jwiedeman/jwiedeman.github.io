# YouTube 6-Second Bumper Ads

YouTube bumper ads are non-skippable video ads of up to six seconds that play before, during, or after a video. Because they cannot be skipped and are very short, viewers see the whole ad and are less annoyed by it. Bumpers build awareness through repetition, much like a billboard.

## How it works

Six seconds is too short for a story or an argument, and that is the point. Each bumper should leave one thing in memory: a brand name, a tagline, a benefit, or a product image. The creative rule is one message, one visual, one audio cue, with no wasted frames. Bumpers work best to reinforce a brand people already know, tease longer content, or stay visible during busy periods.

## How to do it

1. Choose one message per bumper: a brand name, tagline, benefit, or product shot.
2. Show the brand in the first two seconds. Use bold visuals, short text, and your audio signature.
3. Make three to five variations that each cover a different side of the same message.
4. In Google Ads, create a Video campaign with an awareness or reach goal and select bumper ads. Target by demographics, interests, or custom segments.
5. Set a frequency cap and check reach and frequency each week.
6. If you have the budget, run a Brand Lift study to measure recall.

## What to measure

- **Unique reach**: number of unique users who saw at least one bumper.
- **Average frequency**: impressions divided by unique reach, per week.
- **Ad recall lift**: difference in ad recall between exposed and control groups in a Brand Lift study.

## Best practices

- Show the brand early and keep it on screen.
- Run bumpers with longer skippable ads so the bumpers reinforce the longer message.
- Make each bumper work on its own and as part of the series.

## Common pitfalls

- Trying to tell a full story in six seconds.
- Judging bumpers on clicks or conversions. The format is built for awareness and recall.
- Running at frequency too low for repetition to build memory.
