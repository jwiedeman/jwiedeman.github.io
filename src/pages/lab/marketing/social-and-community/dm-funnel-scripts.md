# DM Funnel Scripts

DM funnel scripts are conversation outlines for qualifying leads in direct messages on Instagram, LinkedIn, X, and Facebook Messenger. Instead of improvising each DM, you follow a short flow: confirm fit, build rapport, find the need, and present the right offer in 3 to 5 messages. Paired with a keyword trigger (someone comments a word and gets an automatic first DM), it turns one-to-one selling into a repeatable channel.

## How it works

DMs are more personal than email and less formal than a sales call. That makes people more willing to share their real needs and objections. A DM script applies the steps of a sales conversation (open, qualify, diagnose, offer, close) to casual messaging. The script has to read like a person typing. Stiff, robotic messages fail.

## How to do it

1. Start from an inbound trigger, not cold outreach. Examples: "Comment GUIDE to get the checklist," a story poll response, or a reply to a story call to action.
2. Write the flow:
   - Message 1, deliver: "Hey [Name], here's the guide [link]. Quick question: are you [persona A] or [persona B]?"
   - Message 2, qualify: "Are you currently [doing the thing your product helps with]?"
   - Message 3, diagnose: "Most [persona A] I talk to struggle with [pain]. Is that what you're running into?"
   - Message 4, offer: "We have a [resource or product] for exactly that. Want the details?"
   - Message 5, close: send the link, booking page, or offer.
3. Automate the trigger and first message with a tool such as ManyChat (Instagram, Facebook). Hand the conversation to a person from message 2 onward.
4. Write 3 to 5 script variants for your main segments and entry points. A beginner-tips commenter needs a different conversation than an advanced one.
5. Log every conversation. Track replies, qualified leads, and conversions per script.
6. A/B test qualifying questions, message length, and calls to action.

## What to measure

- **DM response rate**: divide replies to message 1 by first messages sent.
- **Qualified-to-conversion rate**: divide conversions (purchase, booked call, signup) by qualified leads.
- **Revenue per conversation**: divide revenue from DM leads by conversations started.

## Best practices

- Keep each message to three sentences or fewer, with one question at a time.
- Use the person's name and mention the post they engaged with.
- Deliver what you promised before asking any qualifying question.

## Common pitfalls

- Unsolicited DMs with no trigger. Cold DMs from brand accounts read as spam. Base every flow on something the person did.
- Pitching too early. Jumping from "here's the guide" to "buy now" in two messages feels like an ambush.
- No human in the loop. Automation can't handle odd replies or objections. Automate the trigger and first message, and let people handle the rest.
