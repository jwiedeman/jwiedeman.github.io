# Duets & Remixes

TikTok Duets and Instagram Remix let you post your video next to someone else's: reacting, adding commentary, demonstrating, or joking about it. For brands, this means joining content that is already performing instead of starting from a blank page. The format reads as conversation rather than broadcast.

## How it works

A duet is a visible exchange between two creators. People who follow the original video can find yours through it. It also removes the hardest part of content creation, which is coming up with an idea. Brand duets work best in four forms: expert reactions (correcting or adding context), product responses (showing your product solving a problem someone described), humor, and challenge entries.

## How to do it

1. Monitor industry hashtags, competitors, and relevant creators daily. Save videos you can respond to: wrong advice, open questions, product complaints, or trends your product fits.
2. Pick an angle: expert reaction, product response, humor, or challenge entry.
3. Film on a phone with real team members. Keep reactions natural. Skip scripts and studio lighting.
4. Check that your half reads well in the split-screen layout. Keep your face or product clearly visible. Add a text overlay if your point needs emphasis.
5. Use the green-screen format if you want the original video as your background.
6. After posting, tag the original creator and comment on their video pointing to your response.
7. Reply to comments on your duet.

## What to measure

- **Completion rate**: compare watch-to-end rates on duets against your regular posts.
- **Non-follower reach**: check the platform's traffic-source analytics for views from outside your follower base.
- **Follows per post**: compare new followers gained per duet against per original post.

## Best practices

- Let the original video set up the moment and use your half for the payoff or answer.
- Turn on duets and remixes for your own content so customers can respond to you.
- Respond while the original video is still getting views, ideally within a day or two.

## Common pitfalls

- Duetting creators who don't want brand attention. Check whether they have asked brands not to.
- Making your half an ad. Lead with the reaction, humor, or expertise, and let the product appear naturally.
- Only responding to positive content. Answering a complaint or criticism in a duet shows confidence.
