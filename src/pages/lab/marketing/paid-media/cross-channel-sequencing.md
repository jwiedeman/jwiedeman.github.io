# Cross-Channel Sequencing

Cross-channel sequencing plans your ads across platforms so each one moves the buyer forward. For example: a video on TikTok introduces the product, a testimonial on Instagram builds trust, and a branded search ad captures the sale. Each step should add something new instead of repeating the same "Shop Now" message.

## How it works

Buyers usually see a brand several times, on several platforms, before they buy. Without a plan, those touches are random and repetitive. Sequencing treats them as steps in one story. It depends on shared audience data, from pixels, a CDP, or platform audiences, that tells you what a person has already seen. One platform builds awareness, another builds consideration, and another drives the conversion.

## How to do it

1. Map the paths buyers take today. Use GA4 conversion paths, CDP journey reports, or platform assisted-conversion data.
2. Give each platform a role. Example: TikTok or YouTube for awareness, Meta for consideration, display for retargeting, search for capture, email for the close.
3. Define audience stages and share them across platforms. Example stages: "viewed awareness video," "visited site," "viewed product or added to cart." Push these segments to each platform.
4. Make creative for each stage in each platform's native style. Keep the story the same and the format native.
5. Measure the whole sequence, not just the last click. Use GA4 data-driven attribution, an attribution tool, or lift tests.
6. Cap frequency across platforms so the same person does not see too many ads in a week.

## What to measure

- **Multi-platform conversion share**: the percentage of conversions with touchpoints on more than one platform.
- **Touchpoints to conversion**: the average number of ad touches before a conversion, for people who followed the sequence versus those who did not.
- **Incremental value per platform**: each platform's lift from holdout or lift tests, not its last-click credit.

## Best practices

- Start with two platforms, such as YouTube awareness into Google Search capture, before adding more.
- Use consistent UTM parameters and event tracking so you can see the real paths buyers take.
- Keep visuals and message consistent across platforms so people recognize the brand.

## Common pitfalls

- Over-planning the sequence. Real journeys are messy, and a rigid seven-step path fits very few people.
- Ignoring total frequency. Several ads on each platform in one week adds up to too many.
- Teams working in silos. Platform teams must share audiences and creative plans for sequencing to work.
